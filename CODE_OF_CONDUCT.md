# Code of Conduct

Oncefold is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically rigorous
community. Participants must avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
and deliberate disruption. Disagreements should focus on evidence, design, and
behavior rather than people.

Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when conduct
harms the community. Report concerns privately through the repository's
maintainer or security channel; do not include secrets or personal data in a
public issue.
